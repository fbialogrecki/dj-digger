dj-digger — desktop preview / wersja testowa

ENGLISH — GETTING STARTED

Requires macOS 15 or newer. Choose arm64 for Apple Silicon (M-series),
x86_64 for Intel. Python, FFmpeg and the browser components are included.

Not sure which Mac you have? Open Apple menu > About This Mac. An Apple M-series
chip needs arm64; an Intel processor needs x86_64.

1. Drag dj-digger.app onto Applications and wait for the copy to finish.
2. Eject the dj-digger disk image in Finder.
3. Open Finder > Applications and double-click dj-digger.

IF YOUR MAC SHOWS A SECURITY WARNING

dj-digger is a free, open-source hobby project. This preview has no verified
Developer ID signature or Apple notarization. Signing involves costs and
administration the project has not taken on for these previews, so macOS may
say it cannot verify the developer or check the app for malicious software.
Open source does not guarantee a download is safe. Only continue if you trust
the project and downloaded the app from github.com/fbialogrecki/dj-digger.

For an unidentified-developer or missing-notarization warning:
1. Dismiss the warning after trying to open dj-digger from Applications.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Find the message about dj-digger and choose Open Anyway, if available.
4. Confirm when prompted; your Mac may ask for your password or Touch ID.

This approves this app only. Keep your Mac's security protections on.
If macOS reports malware, damage, or that the app will harm your computer,
stop and report the exact message instead of bypassing security checks.

FULL GUIDE AND HELP

https://github.com/fbialogrecki/dj-digger/blob/main/docs/installation.md#mac

This is a preview and may have bugs. Updates are manual: close dj-digger,

--- Polski ---

Wymaga macOS 15 lub nowszego. Wybierz arm64 dla Apple Silicon (seria M),
x86_64 dla Intela. Python, FFmpeg i komponenty przeglądarki są dołączone.

Otwórz DMG i przeciągnij dj-digger.app na Applications (Aplikacje).
Wysuń obraz dysku i uruchom dj-digger z folderu Aplikacje. Terminal nie jest
potrzebny.

Wersja testowa nie ma podpisu Developer ID ani notaryzacji Apple. macOS może
zablokować pierwsze uruchomienie. Jeżeli ufasz pobranemu plikowi, skorzystaj
z Ustawienia systemowe > Prywatność i ochrona > Otwórz mimo to dla tej aplikacji.
Nie wyłączaj zabezpieczeń systemu. Automatyczne aktualizacje nie są dostępne.
